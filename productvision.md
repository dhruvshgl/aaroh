# Aaroh — AI Career Consultant

### Your career, understood. Your next step, defined.

## 1. What is Aaroh?

**Aaroh is an AI-powered career consultant that analyzes a person's current career profile, understands their aspirations, identifies the gap between where they are and where they want to be, and creates a personalized path to get there.**

Aaroh combines information from a user's:

* Resume
* Education
* Work experience
* Technical and professional skills
* Projects
* Achievements
* Current role
* Interests
* Career aspirations
* Self-described strengths and weaknesses

to build a deeper understanding of the individual.

Instead of simply reviewing a resume or recommending courses, Aaroh answers a much more important question:

> **"Given who I am today, where can I go, what is standing between me and that destination, and what should I do next?"**

---

# 2. The Problem

Career decisions are often made with incomplete information.

A student may know:

> "I want to work in AI."

But not know:

* Which AI role?
* Whether they are currently suited for it
* What skills they are missing
* How much they already know
* What they should learn first
* Which projects would actually help
* What companies expect from someone at their level
* Whether another career path may suit them better

Similarly, a software engineer may say:

> "I want to become a senior engineer."

But that doesn't tell them:

* What separates their current level from senior level
* Which technical skills they need
* Which engineering competencies they lack
* Whether they need system design, leadership, architecture, or domain depth
* What evidence they need to demonstrate those capabilities

Most career platforms solve only a small part of this problem.

**Resume builders optimize the resume.**

**Job portals find jobs.**

**Course platforms provide learning material.**

**Generic AI assistants answer questions.**

Aaroh brings these pieces together around one central objective:

> **Understand the person and help them navigate their career.**

---

# 3. The Core Philosophy

Aaroh is built around a simple model:

### Current State → Desired State → Gap → Path → Progress

Aaroh first understands:

### Where are you?

Your current skills, experience, education, projects, strengths and career history.

Then:

### Where do you want to go?

Your desired role, industry, level, company type, domain or broader career ambition.

Then:

### What is missing?

The skills, experience, knowledge, projects, credentials and competencies that separate your current profile from the target.

Then:

### How do you get there?

A personalized roadmap containing what to learn, what to build, what experience to gain and what milestones to achieve.

Finally:

### Are you progressing?

Aaroh can continuously reassess the user's profile as they learn, build projects, gain experience and change their goals.

---

# 4. What the User Gives Aaroh

Aaroh begins by building a **Career Profile**.

## Resume

The user can upload their existing resume.

Aaroh extracts:

* Education
* Work experience
* Internships
* Projects
* Technical skills
* Soft skills
* Certifications
* Achievements
* Domains
* Responsibilities
* Career progression

The resume is treated as **evidence**, not as the complete definition of the person.

---

## Self-Assessment

The user can explain themselves in their own words.

For example:

> "I'm a frontend developer. I enjoy building products but I don't enjoy pixel-perfect UI work as much. I like solving technical problems and understanding how systems work."

This information can reveal things that a resume cannot.

---

## Career Aspiration

The user describes what they want.

This can range from highly specific:

> "I want to become an AI Engineer at a product company."

to relatively vague:

> "I want to move into AI."

or completely open-ended:

> "I don't know what I want to do next."

Aaroh should work with all three.

---

### Career Profile

**Secondary Skills**

**Career Interests**

AI, backend engineering and system architecture.

---

# 6. Career Diagnosis

Aaroh then analyzes the user's current position.

It identifies:

### Strengths

Capabilities where the user already demonstrates meaningful evidence.

### Weaknesses

Areas where the user may need development.

### Skill Depth

Not just whether a skill appears on a resume, but the apparent depth of experience with that skill.

For example:

> Python — Listed on resume

is different from:

> Python — Used to build production ML pipelines for 18 months.

### Experience Depth

Aaroh evaluates the quality and relevance of the user's experience against their desired direction.

### Transferable Skills

Capabilities that can carry into another role.

For example:

A frontend engineer may have:

* Product sense
* User experience understanding
* API knowledge
* JavaScript expertise

which can contribute to a transition toward full-stack or product-oriented roles.

---

For example:

### Professional capabilities

* Problem solving

---

# 8. The Career Gap

This is one of Aaroh's most important outputs.

Instead of simply saying:

> "Learn machine learning."

Aaroh explains:

### You already have

* Python fundamentals
* API development
* Software engineering experience
* Cloud deployment

### You need to strengthen

* Statistics
* ML fundamentals
* Model evaluation
* Data pipelines

### You have limited evidence of

* Production ML systems
* MLOps
* Model serving

This produces a **personalized skill gap**, rather than a generic curriculum.

---

# 9. Alternative Career Discovery

Aaroh does not blindly follow the user's stated career goal.

It can identify **adjacent career paths** that appear relevant based on the user's profile.

For example, someone might enter:

> "I want to become a Machine Learning Engineer."

Aaroh may identify other potentially relevant paths such as:

* Data Scientist
* AI Engineer
* Data Engineer
* Applied AI Engineer
* ML Platform Engineer

The important part is that Aaroh explains the reasoning.

For example:

> "Your current software engineering experience gives you a strong foundation for AI engineering. Your profile currently contains less evidence of the mathematical and statistical depth commonly associated with traditional ML roles."

The purpose isn't to tell the user what career they should choose.

The purpose is to **expand their awareness of viable paths**.

---

# 10. Career Pathways

Aaroh can represent career progression as a pathway.

For example:

**Current**

Frontend Engineer

↓

**Near-term**

Full-Stack Engineer

↓

**Mid-term**

Senior Full-Stack Engineer

↓

**Long-term**

Staff Engineer / Engineering Lead

Each transition can have its own requirements.

This allows Aaroh to answer:

> "What should I do next?"

rather than overwhelming the user with a five-year plan.

---

# 11. Personalized Learning Roadmap

Aaroh converts the identified gaps into an actionable roadmap.

Instead of:

> Learn Python → Learn ML → Learn Deep Learning

the roadmap becomes:

### Phase 1 — Foundations

**Duration:** 3–4 weeks

Focus:

* Python for data/ML
* Probability
* Statistics
* Linear algebra

### Phase 2 — Machine Learning

**Duration:** 4–6 weeks

Focus:

* Supervised learning
* Unsupervised learning
* Model evaluation
* Feature engineering

### Phase 3 — Applied AI

**Duration:** 4–6 weeks

Focus:

* LLM APIs
* Embeddings
* RAG
* Evaluation
* AI application architecture

### Phase 4 — Production

**Duration:** 4–8 weeks

Focus:

* Model/API deployment
* Cloud
* Monitoring
* Scaling
* MLOps

---

# 12. Projects as Proof of Ability

Aaroh should not stop at learning.

It should answer:

> **"What can I build to prove that I know this?"**

For each skill gap, Aaroh can recommend projects.

For example:

### Project 1

**Build an end-to-end RAG application**

Demonstrates:

* Embeddings
* Vector databases
* Retrieval
* LLM integration
* Evaluation
* Backend engineering

### Project 2

**Build a production ML prediction service**

Demonstrates:

* Model training
* Model serving
* APIs
* Docker
* Cloud deployment

This makes the roadmap **evidence-driven**.

---

# 13. From Learning to Employability

Eventually Aaroh should connect:

**Skill → Learning → Project → Experience → Resume Evidence → Job**

For example:

```text
Learn RAG
    ↓
Build RAG project
    ↓
Deploy it
    ↓
Measure performance
    ↓
Document architecture
    ↓
Add meaningful evidence to resume
    ↓
Apply to relevant AI Engineer roles
```

This makes Aaroh a career execution platform rather than merely a career advice platform.

---

# 14. Career Readiness

Aaroh can maintain a dynamic view of how prepared a user is for a particular career direction.

For example:

### AI Engineer

**Current alignment**

Strong

**Key gaps**

* ML fundamentals
* Evaluation
* Production AI architecture

**Evidence needed**

* 2 production-quality AI projects
* AI system design experience
* Deployment experience

The readiness assessment should be based on observable evidence and clearly explain what contributes to it.

---

# 15. Market Intelligence

A future version of Aaroh can incorporate real-world job market data.

Instead of relying entirely on generic knowledge, Aaroh can analyze current job descriptions for the user's target:

* Roles
* Companies
* Experience levels
* Skills
* Technologies
* Responsibilities
* Qualifications

It can then identify patterns.

For example:

> "Among the roles you've selected, Docker, cloud deployment and system design appear frequently. Your profile currently provides limited evidence for these areas."

This creates a bridge between:

**What the user wants**

and

**What the market currently asks for.**

---

# 16. The AI Career Consultant

The report is only the beginning.

Aaroh should also have a conversational interface.

The user can ask:

> "Why did you say I'm weak in system design?"

> "What if I don't want to learn Kubernetes?"

> "Would I be better suited for AI engineering or data science?"

> "Can you redesign my roadmap if I only have 10 hours per week?"

> "I just completed the project you recommended. What should I do now?"

> "Review this job description against my profile."

Aaroh should maintain context about the user's career journey and respond as an ongoing consultant.

How the career profile changes over time.

---

# 18. The Long-Term Vision

The ultimate vision for Aaroh is not:

> **"AI that reviews your resume."**

It is:

> **"AI that understands your career."**

Aaroh can eventually become a persistent career intelligence layer around a person.

The user might return six months later and say:

> "I've completed the roadmap."

Aaroh can reassess them.

The user changes jobs:

> "Here's my new role."

Aaroh updates their career profile.

The user discovers a new interest:

> "I think I want to move into product management."

Aaroh evaluates the transition.

The user finds a job:

> "Here's the job description."

Aaroh compares it with their profile.

Over time, Aaroh becomes a **living model of the user's professional development**.
